OG image source files.

Each *.svg here is rendered to /public/og/<name>.png at commit time via:
    convert -density 144 -background none src/<name>.svg <name>.png

The PNG is what we actually serve as og:image (modern crawlers handle SVG
inconsistently; PNG is universally supported). Keep the SVG as the source
of truth — when the design needs updating, edit the SVG and re-run the
conversion.

Design language:
- 1200×630 (Open Graph + Twitter summary_large_image standard)
- Dark navy ground (#0f172a) for editorial weight
- Site accent crimson (#7a1e1e) for the brand stripe + seal
- "CYBER · TRACKR" wordmark top-left with a "CT" seal
- Page title in serif at ~76px (2-line max)
- Subtitle in sans-serif at ~30px (2-line max)
- Small "cyber.trackr.live/<path>" hint bottom-left
- Right-side accent column with a page-specific glyph
